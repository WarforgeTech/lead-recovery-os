# Pipeline Recovery OS Mortgage Pitch Flow

## One-sentence explanation

Pipeline Recovery OS turns a broker or loan officer's stale leads, unfinished applications, past clients, and referral relationships into a daily human-approved follow-up queue.

The product is not meant to replace the CRM. It sits on top of the CRM data after a white-glove import and tells the team what to work next, why it matters, and what message to review before they contact the person.

## The simple daily flow

For the broker or loan officer:

1. Open the Dashboard and check the goal gap.
2. Work the Pipeline and Rescue queues for the highest-value borrower conversations.
3. Open a lead, review the recommended follow-up, edit if needed, and save the status.
4. Export approved follow-ups or use them manually in the normal phone, email, or text workflow.
5. Review the Rollup at the end of the week to see what moved.

For the assistant:

1. Open Tasks.
2. Clear missing-document, signature, application-help, and overdue follow-up items.
3. Escalate only the leads that need the loan officer directly.
4. Keep the queue clean so the broker is not digging through the CRM.

For the broker owner or CEO:

1. Open Rollup.
2. See application creation, stale records recovered, follow-ups completed, active pipeline value, and where the pipeline leaks.
3. Decide whether the pilot is producing enough recovered conversations to expand.

## The airplane / between-calls version

"You do not need to live in this all day. In between calls, open the app, go to Queue or Rescue, pick the next highest-priority person, review the message, edit if needed, and save the status. If you are driving, the assistant works the queue and only escalates the people who need you directly."

## The clean X, Y, Z pitch

"After we import the contacts, your day-to-day is simple:

X: Check the dashboard to see the goal gap and follow-ups due.

Y: Work the Pipeline and Rescue queues, starting with the highest-score borrowers.

Z: Approve or edit the follow-up draft, contact them through your normal channel, and update the status.

Your assistant handles the task queue. You handle the conversations that need your voice. The broker owner watches the rollup."

## Why this is valuable

- Old contacts are already paid for, already earned, or already referred.
- Application-started and talked-no-application borrowers are warmer than cold leads.
- 14/21-day falloff creates predictable leakage.
- Assistants can remove friction without pretending to be the loan officer.
- Human-approved drafts make the system useful without creating mass-send compliance risk.
- The pilot proves whether the database contains recoverable conversations before a bigger rollout.

## Compliance posture

- No outbound sending in v1.
- No mass SMS/email claim.
- Drafts require human review.
- Consent/contact-risk is shown per lead.
- Referral partner language avoids referral-compensation promises.
- Demo and reviewer data are synthetic.
