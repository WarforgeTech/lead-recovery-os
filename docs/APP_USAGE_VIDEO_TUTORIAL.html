The story to tell on video

"Pipeline Recovery OS is not trying to replace a CRM. It is the recovery layer for leads that already fell out of the day-to-day sales process. A loan officer gets referrals from text messages, Facebook, email, open houses, and old CRM exports. The problem is not that they do not know how to sell. The problem is that hot people disappear after one or two touches. This system imports the messy data, scrubs out closed or do-not-contact records, merges duplicate touchpoints into one journey, and turns the remaining people into a daily follow-up queue with human-approved messages."

Business problem

Hot leads go cold because the team cannot remember every referral, app link, partial application, declined file, and dead deal.

Product answer

Bring messy old data into one reviewable queue, then tell the user who to contact today, why, and what to say.

Safety posture

No outbound sending in v1. Suppression rules happen before drafts. Human acceptance gates every import.

Processing screen narration

Queued

The job is created in Supabase and waiting for the worker.

Archived

The raw input is stored privately for audit and future reprocessing.

Reading file

The worker downloads the raw file and detects the source format.

Parsing

CSV, text, spreadsheet, JSON, VCF, DOCX, or text PDF data is turned into raw rows.

Normalizing

Names, phones, emails, dates, statuses, notes, and sources are cleaned.

Removing duplicates

Repeated touchpoints are grouped into one lead journey where possible.

Scrubbing exclusions

DNC, opt-out, closed, funded elsewhere, and invalid records are held out before drafts are created.

Reconstructing journeys

AI can be used for ambiguous notes when configured. Otherwise deterministic fallback keeps the import moving.

Ready for review

The import is staged. Nothing becomes an active contact until the user accepts it.

How to explain the final numbers

Ready

Good enough to become active contacts and opportunities after acceptance.

Suppressed

Excluded because of DNC, closed/dead status, bad contact, or a prior suppression match.

Merged

Rows from the second import matched existing contacts and extended the journey instead of duplicating people.

Presenter script for the daily workflow

"After onboarding, the day-to-day is simple. Adam or Janine opens Today. They do not go hunt through a CRM. The system gives them the people most likely to disappear today, shows why the person is on the list, gives them a suggested follow-up, and asks them to record the outcome. If nobody replies, the cadence schedules the next touch. If the borrower replies, the no-response cadence stops. If an application is submitted, that person moves out of recovery. If Janine needs Adam, she escalates it with one click. At the end of the day, if she has a messy recap, she pastes it into the end-of-day update box and the system applies the matched updates."
